Base: release/staging-deployment
Head: codex/auth-security-review

through URLs and frontend storage. OAuth callbacks now require the initiating

cookies, and exact frontend-origin checks protect redirects/CORS/cookie writes.

and removal are rechecked. First-time Google/GitHub provisioning now sets the new
user's transaction-local RLS identity before creating the initial owner workspace.
